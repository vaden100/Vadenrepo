## Our goal

Everyone should be able to use this site. We aim for WCAG 2.2 level AA, and we go further on text contrast (7 to 1 for body text).

## What we do

- We test every page with tools that check for barriers. We test light, dark and high contrast.
- The site works with a keyboard, screen readers, zoom up to 400 percent and Windows high contrast.
- You can change text size, spacing, motion and colors. Use **Reading settings** at the bottom of each page.
- We write in plain language.

## Known gaps

- The logo is a stand-in until we have the final file.
- Some details on the site are still marked as placeholders.
- We have not yet tested with people who use assistive technology every day. We plan to.

## Tell us about a problem

If something does not work for you, tell us and we will fix it. You do not need an email account: you can text us from our current website, linked on the [contact page](/contact).
