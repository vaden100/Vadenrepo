## Cookies

This website sets no cookies.

## Browser storage

If you change **Reading settings**, your choices are saved in your browser's local storage under the name `ns1-reading`. They never leave your device. Use **Reset settings** to clear them, or clear your browser's site data.

## When this changes

If we ever need a cookie, we will list it here first with its name, purpose and how long it lasts. We will not use cookies for advertising.
