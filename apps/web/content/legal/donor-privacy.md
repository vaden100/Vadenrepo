## Our promise

We will never sell, rent or trade the names or details of people who give to us.

## How giving will work

Online giving is not open yet. When it opens, your card details will be handled by a donation platform, never by this website. This page will name that platform and link to its privacy policy.

## What we will keep

Your name, contact details, gift amounts and dates, so we can thank you, send receipts and keep honest records. Only staff who need it will see it.

## Your choices

You can ask to give anonymously, to stop hearing from us, or to see, correct or delete your details. Use the [contact page](/contact).
