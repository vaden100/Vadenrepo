## What this website collects today

- **Nothing you type.** The site has no forms yet.
- **No tracking.** We use no analytics, no ads, no tracking pixels and no session recording.
- **Reading settings** (text size, spacing, motion, contrast, theme) are saved in your own browser only. We never see them. See [cookies and storage](/legal/cookies).
- **Server logs.** Like every website, our hosting provider may keep short technical logs (such as the page asked for and the time) to keep the site safe and working.

## When this changes

We will add forms later, for example to sign up or to give. Before a form goes live, this page will say what it collects and why. It will also say how long we keep it and who can see it.
